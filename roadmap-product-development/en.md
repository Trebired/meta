Work on Operlorn and Sessionary stopped while the organization and its site were written. That was deliberate: there was no point shipping products from an organization that could not yet say what it stood for.

Both resume once the initial release is out. They are open source on the day they ship, like everything else here, and neither gets announced before there is something to run.
