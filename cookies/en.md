## The short version

Trebired sets no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies, and no cross-site identifiers. Nothing on our sites follows you anywhere else.

The only storage we use is what a page needs to work, and it stays in your browser.

## What we store

A chosen interface language, so reloading a page does not throw you back to the default.

Short-lived session state while you are signed in to a product that requires an account.

Nothing else. We store no visitor identifier, and we build no profile from what you read.

## What we do not store

We set no cookie for advertising, retargeting, conversion tracking, attribution, audience building, or analytics of any kind, first-party or third-party.

We do not fingerprint a device, and we do not reconstruct an identity from the properties of a browser.

## Third parties

Our marketing sites load no third-party scripts, so no third party can set a cookie through us.

Where a product embeds something you asked for, such as a map, that embed is named on the page before it loads.

## Clearing what we store

Clearing site data in your browser removes everything we hold on your device. Nothing we keep elsewhere depends on it, so clearing it costs you nothing beyond a language choice.
