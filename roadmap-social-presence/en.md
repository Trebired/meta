The organization holds its name on the sites where software is discussed, and posts there: releases, decisions with their reasons, and the occasional account of something that went wrong. The same material as the blog, carried to where people already are.

An account is a place to announce, not a channel to perform on. Nothing is posted because a schedule says so, no engagement is bought, and anything said there is said in full somewhere the organization owns.
